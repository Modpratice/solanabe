The game is ready

Your hive, from the first bee to the mythic

A bee strategy game on Solana, in your desktop or phone browser.

Farm all day

Your bees farm nectar, and with the butler the hive works 24 h, even while you are away.

Defend the door

Guards at the entrance hold off the wasps, ants and hornets that come for your honey.

Champions tournament

Every day, the champion of each hive fights in her rarity division, and the top 4 split the pot.

3 species

Which bee is yours?

One species per hive, and you pick it in the kit. Each one takes up a different amount of comb, so more or fewer bees fit.

  • European balanced 40 fit in a 1,000-cell comb.
  • Jataí defensive Tiny: 66 fit in the same comb.
  • Bumblebee offensive Big: 20 fit in the same comb.
